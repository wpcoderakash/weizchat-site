# Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how to have personal data deleted from WeizChat. It is a permanent,
public URL: `https://weiz.chat/data-deletion`.

## If you are a business using WeizChat

1. Sign in at [app.weiz.chat](https://app.weiz.chat/login) and use the deletion options
   in **Settings**, or
2. Email [office@weiz.co.il](mailto:office@weiz.co.il) from the email address of an
   organization administrator, stating what you want deleted — a single customer record,
   a conversation, or the entire account.

We verify the request, delete the data within **30 days**, and confirm in writing.
Backups that include the data expire on their own fixed schedule; deleted data is not
restored from backup into live systems. Records we must keep by law (for example,
invoices) are retained only as long as the law requires.

## If you are a customer of a business that uses WeizChat

The business you spoke with on WhatsApp is the controller of your conversation data. Ask
that business to delete your data — or write to
[office@weiz.co.il](mailto:office@weiz.co.il) with the business name and the phone
number you wrote from, and we will forward the request to the business and assist it in
fulfilling the deletion.

## What gets deleted

- Your WhatsApp conversation history with the business, including media.
- Your customer record: phone number, profile name, custom fields, tags and notes.
- For a full account deletion: all organization data, including catalog content, AI
  configuration, campaign history and member accounts.

## What does not disappear

Copies of the conversation on your own WhatsApp app, and data held independently by
Meta, are outside WeizChat's systems — Meta's own
[deletion instructions](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/) apply to those.
