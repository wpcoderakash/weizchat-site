# Accessibility Statement

`__LEGAL_NAME__` is committed to making the WeizChat website and application usable by
everyone, including people with disabilities, in accordance with the Israeli Equal
Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, the Service Accessibility Regulations
(5773-2013), Israeli Standard **IS 5568** and **WCAG 2.1 level AA**.

## What is implemented on this site

- Semantic HTML landmarks, headings and lists; forms with programmatic labels.
- Full keyboard operability, with a visible focus indicator on every interactive
  element.
- Colour tokens chosen for AA contrast in both light and dark mode.
- Right-to-left and left-to-right layouts built with logical CSS properties, so Hebrew
  is a first-class direction, not a patched one.
- Motion is reduced for users whose system requests reduced motion.
- Images carry text alternatives; decorative graphics are hidden from assistive
  technology.
- The site is responsive and usable at 200% zoom and on small screens.
