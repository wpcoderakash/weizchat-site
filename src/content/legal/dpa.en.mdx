# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This addendum forms part of the agreement between `__LEGAL_NAME__` ("processor", "we")
and the business using WeizChat ("controller", "you"), and applies wherever we process
personal data on your behalf.

## 1. Subject matter and instructions

We process the personal data of your customers — WhatsApp messages and media, phone
numbers, profile names, and the CRM records you keep — solely to provide the WeizChat
service, on your documented instructions, and never for our own purposes. Using the
product's features constitutes your instruction to process the data those features
require.

## 2. Duration

For the term of your agreement with us. On termination we delete or return the data as
described in section 7.

## 3. Confidentiality

Personnel with access to personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations and
access data only where their role requires it.

## 4. Security

We apply the technical and organisational measures described on the
[security page](/security), including encryption in transit, envelope encryption of
credentials, per-tenant isolation enforced in the database, separated administrative
planes, audit logging and redacted-by-default logs. We will not lower the overall level
of protection during the term.

## 5. Sub-processors

You authorise the sub-processors listed in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy)
(currently: Meta Platforms for WhatsApp message delivery, Anthropic for AI reply
generation, and `__HOSTING_PROVIDER__` for hosting). We will announce any intended
addition or replacement in advance and you may object on reasonable grounds. We remain
responsible for our sub-processors' performance.

## 6. Assistance

We will assist you, at your reasonable request, with data-subject requests (access,
correction, deletion, portability), with security-incident notification duties, and with
data-protection impact assessments, taking into account the nature of the processing.

## 7. Deletion and return

On termination, or on your instruction, we delete the personal data we process for you
within 30 days, unless law requires us to keep it longer. You can export your data
before closing the account.

## 8. Breach notification

We will notify you without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach
affecting your data, with the information you need for your own legal obligations.

## 9. International transfers

Where processing involves a transfer out of Israel or the EEA, it takes place under a
recognised transfer mechanism (such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual
clauses). Details of processing regions: `__HOSTING_REGION__`.

## 10. Audit

Once per year, on reasonable notice and under confidentiality, you may verify our
compliance with this addendum through documentation we provide or, where that is
insufficient, an audit that does not endanger the data of other tenants.
