## **What WeizChat Is, and the Two Roles We Act In**

WeizChat is a software platform for managing business conversations on WhatsApp: a shared team inbox, an AI assistant, chatbot and automation tools, template-based campaigns and customer management. The Platform connects to the **WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API)** operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta"). We are an independent software provider and are not affiliated with Meta.

We process personal information in two distinct roles:

- **As a controller** — for information about visitors to the Website, people who contact us, and the people who open and operate WeizChat accounts. This Policy describes that processing.
- **As a processor on behalf of our business customers** — for the conversations, contact details and related content of the **customers of the businesses** that use WeizChat. Each business is the controller of its own customers' data and instructs us to process it in order to provide the service, under this Policy. If you are a customer of a business that uses WeizChat, please direct privacy requests to that business; we will assist it in fulfilling them, as described on the [data deletion page](/data-deletion).

## **WhatsApp and the Cloud API**

Messages sent and received through the Platform are routed through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). Meta processes such messages in accordance with its own terms and policies, including its data-retention practices for the Cloud API, which are outside the Company's control. Copies of conversations held on your own WhatsApp application, and data held independently by Meta, are outside WeizChat's systems.

Within WeizChat, conversation content is stored for the business that owns the conversation, so that its team can read, manage and answer it — which is the service itself.

## **AI Processing**

Where a business enables the AI assistant, chatbot or related features, conversation content and the business's configured catalog and rules are processed to generate replies and summaries. For this purpose we use carefully selected third-party AI model providers (such as Anthropic) as sub-processors, bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations; we do not use our customers' content to train models of our own.

Each business controls these features: it can choose a draft-only mode in which a human approves every reply, pause the assistant, or take over any conversation with a human agent at any time.

## **Retention and Deletion**

We retain personal information for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected — as a rule, for as long as the relevant account is active — and thereafter as required to comply with law, resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

A business may delete individual customer records, conversations or its entire account, or request deletion by email; verified deletion requests are fulfilled **within 30 days** and confirmed in writing, as described on the [data deletion page](/data-deletion). Backups that include deleted data expire on their own fixed schedule and are not restored into live systems. Records that must be kept by law (for example, invoices) are retained only as long as the law requires.
