# Privacy Policy

**Who we are.** WeizChat is operated by `__LEGAL_NAME__` (company no. `__COMPANY_ID__`),
`__ADDRESS__`. Questions about this policy: [office@weiz.co.il](mailto:office@weiz.co.il).

WeizChat is a customer-conversation platform for businesses on the WhatsApp Business
Platform: a shared team inbox, an AI assistant, chatbot and automation tools, campaigns
and a simple CRM. This policy covers both this website (weiz.chat) and the WeizChat
application (app.weiz.chat), and is written to comply with the Israeli Protection of
Privacy Law, 5741-1981 and, where it applies, the EU GDPR.

## The two roles we play

- **For your account** (name, email, phone number, sign-in history, billing details) we
  are the **controller**: we decide what is collected and why.
- **For your customers' conversations** (WhatsApp messages, phone numbers, profile
  names, order details your customers send you) the business using WeizChat is the
  **controller** and we are a **processor**: we store and process that data only to
  provide the service, under the business's instructions and our
  [Data Processing Addendum](/dpa).

## What we process

- **Account data** — name, email, WhatsApp number used for sign-in, role, language,
  session and sign-in records.
- **Conversation data** — messages between a business and its customers on WhatsApp,
  including media, delivery status, and the customer's phone number and WhatsApp profile
  name.
- **Business content** — product catalogs, prices, business facts, AI rules and message
  templates the business configures.
- **Website inquiries** — what you submit through the contact form or a waitlist on
  weiz.chat (name, company, phone, email, your message). We store it to reply and to
  notify you about the feature you asked about, and delete it when the inquiry is
  resolved or on request.
- **Usage and log data** — technical events needed to run, secure and debug the service.
  Logs are redacted by default; message content and credentials are excluded.

We do **not** sell personal data. We do not use conversation data for advertising.

## AI processing

When a business enables the AI assistant, the content of a conversation and the
business's own catalog and rules are sent to our LLM provider (Anthropic) to generate a
reply. Businesses control the autonomy level, including a mode where a human approves
every AI reply. We do not permit our providers to use this data to train their models.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as the account is active. When an account is closed, or on a
verified deletion request, personal data is deleted within 30 days except where the law
requires longer retention (for example, tax records). Full instructions are on our
[data deletion page](/data-deletion) — a stable, public URL.

## Your rights

Subject to applicable law you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a
copy of your personal data, and you may object to certain processing. Write to
[office@weiz.co.il](mailto:office@weiz.co.il). If you are a customer of a business that
uses WeizChat, the business is the controller of your conversation data — we will
forward your request to it and assist with fulfilling it.

## Cookies

The website uses essential cookies only. Optional analytics run **only after** you allow
them in the consent banner; no analytics scripts load before consent.

## Security

Security measures are described on our [security page](/security). In the event of a
breach affecting personal data we will notify the affected businesses and authorities as
the law requires.
